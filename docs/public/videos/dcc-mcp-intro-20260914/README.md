# DCC-MCP introduction video

A one-minute introduction in Chinese and English. The homepage uses the matching
language's landscape edition with native playback controls. Captions are already
visible in the video; optional WebVTT tracks do not enable themselves by default.
The homepage also includes the complete text transcript.

## Files

- `intro.zh.mp4` and `intro.en.mp4`: 60-second landscape editions, with AI male
  narration and same-language captions.
- `poster.zh.webp` and `poster.en.webp`: stills from the corresponding finished film.
- `captions.zh.vtt` and `captions.en.vtt`: narration captions authored through the
  Kdenlive adapter.

## Production and sources

Project: [DCC-MCP Kdenlive](https://github.com/dcc-mcp/dcc-mcp-kdenlive).
The edit combines original Remotion motion graphics, ImageGen decorative art,
existing DCC-MCP project showcases, original generated music and sound effects,
and AI male narration (Yunxi in Chinese and Guy in English).

- [Blender showcase](https://github.com/dcc-mcp/dcc-mcp-blender): procedural galaxy.
- [Houdini showcase](https://github.com/dcc-mcp/dcc-mcp-houdini): procedural portal.
- [Maya architecture](https://github.com/dcc-mcp/dcc-mcp-maya-procedural-architecture):
  editable Bifrost architecture with ambientCG CC0 assets.
- [Marmoset showcase](https://github.com/dcc-mcp/dcc-mcp-marmoset): real Toolbag PBR
  render. Dirty Football by Rohit Seervi / [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/dirty_football), CC0.

The DCC-MCP lockup is reused intact from the website brand assets. Decorative
graphics do not depict product UI. Existing showcase footage is presented as
project examples, not as newly recorded host acceptance.

## Media record

The MP4 files are web derivatives of the finished Kdenlive landscape exports:
1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264 `yuv420p`, BT.709, stereo AAC at 48 kHz, and fast-start
MP4 layout. The picture runs for 60 seconds; the container duration is 60.010
seconds including the encoded audio tail. Each WebP poster is a 1920 × 1080 frame
from its corresponding finished film at 54.5 seconds.
